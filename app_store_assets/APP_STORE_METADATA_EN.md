## 5. Description
*(Apple App Store limit: 4,000 characters maximum — Ready to copy & paste)*

```text
Evernear — Always Near Your Loved Ones, Peace of Mind Preserved.

Evernear is a calm, privacy-first inactivity monitor and daily safety check-in companion designed for independent seniors, solo dwellers, and families living apart. Stay reassured that your loved ones are safe every day—without intrusive cameras, microphones, or continuous background GPS tracking.

WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE EVERNEAR

Living alone or caring for aging parents from afar shouldn't mean choosing between constant anxiety and invasive surveillance. Evernear bridges the gap with Calm Technology: effortless one-tap check-ins, passive phone activity recognition, timezone-aware nighttime Quiet Hours, and an authoritative server safety clock that alerts family guardians only when truly needed.

KEY FEATURES

• One-Tap "I am OK" Safety Check-In
A soothing, oversized check-in button at the center of the home screen lets protected users confirm their well-being in a single tap, immediately resetting the safety countdown and reassuring connected family members.

• Passive Daily Activity Recognition
You don't need to keep the app open all day. Evernear passively recognizes natural routine phone interactions—such as app foreground activity and charger connection events—to confirm daily activity without interrupting your routine.

• Dual-Role Experience in One App
Switch seamlessly between two tailored views inside a single account:
  - Monitored Mode (Protected Person): Minimalist, high-contrast interface with a calm breathing safety shield, battery indicator, and active schedule status.
  - Guardian Mode (Family Caregiver): Real-time family dashboard displaying each relative's protection status, latest confirmation time, operational priority score (0–100), and one-tap incident acknowledgement.

• Smart Quiet Hours (Nighttime Sleep Windows)
Configure daily active monitoring hours and nighttime Quiet Hours (for example, 22:00 to 07:00 in the user's local timezone). The safety timer automatically freezes while your loved ones sleep—preventing false nighttime alarms while continuing to record overnight charging safely.

• Authoritative Server Clock & Signal Separation
Even if a phone battery dies or loses network connectivity, Evernear's central server deterministically evaluates check-in schedules. Technical device reachability pings (Heartbeat = 0.0) are strictly separated from real human activity and explicit check-ins (Weight = 1.0), ensuring a powered-on phone sitting on a table never masks an overdue check-in.

• 48-Hour QR & 6-Character Family Pairing
Connect with parents or relatives in seconds by scanning a secure QR code or entering a 6-character invitation code valid for 48 hours. Monitoring relationships require mutual consent and can be paused or revoked at any time.

• Resilient Offline Sync Queue
If internet connectivity drops, safety check-ins and activity signals are queued locally on the device with cryptographic idempotency keys and synchronized automatically the moment connectivity returns.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

• Zero Cameras & Zero Microphones: Evernear never records photos, videos, or audio. Camera permission is optional and used strictly when scanning a family pairing QR code.
• Zero Continuous Background GPS: Evernear never tracks continuous background location or movement history. Single-shot coordinates are attached only when explicitly enabled and only during a check-in or escalated alert.
• Full Data Sovereignty: Pause monitoring anytime or permanently delete your account and all associated history directly inside the app (Settings → Delete Account).

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

Evernear is a non-diagnostic family check-in and inactivity notification tool. Risk scores (0–100) are operational check-in latency indicators and do not constitute medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or health monitoring. Evernear is not a medical device and does not replace or contact public emergency services (such as 911, 112, 999, or 115). In any medical or life-threatening emergency, always contact local emergency services immediately.
```

```text
Thank you for reviewing Evernear! Below is a quick guide to help you test all core features, app-specific settings, and privacy controls during your review.

1. ACCOUNT ACCESS & QUICK ONBOARDING
• Demo / Test Account (Pre-configured with sample family & activity data):
  - Email: reviewer@evernear.app (or you may register any new email address directly in the app; no external SMS or email OTP barrier is required to complete sign-up).
  - Password: [Your Demo Account Password, e.g., EvernearReview2026!]
• Switching Language to English:
  - Evernear supports 10 languages and automatically detects the device locale. You can also switch languages at any time via:
    - Login / Register screen → Language selector in the top-right corner ("English"), OR
    - Bottom Navigation → Settings → Language → English.

2. HOW TO TEST CORE FEATURES & APP-SPECIFIC SETTINGS

A. One-Tap "I am OK" Safety Check-In (Monitored Mode)
1. Open the "Status" (Home) tab.
2. Tap the large central green shield button ("I am okay — Tap to confirm") or the floating green heart button in the bottom navigation bar.
3. On the Check-In Confirmation screen, you will see your confirmed timestamp, an optional toggle to attach a one-time location snapshot ("Share location in this check-in"), and a button to view Recent Activities.

B. Customizing Monitoring Schedule & Quiet Hours (Sleep Freeze)
1. From the "Status" tab, tap "Set Sleep & Monitoring Schedule" (or go to Settings → Monitoring & reminders).
2. Here you can test app-specific monitoring settings:
   - Active Hours & Quiet Hours / Sleep Window (e.g., Active 07:00–22:00, Sleep 22:00–07:00): During Quiet Hours, inactivity checks are automatically suspended so users are never disturbed while sleeping.
   - Check-in Reminders: Configure up to 3 gentle daily local reminder times (e.g., 09:00, 15:00, 20:00).
   - Pause Mode (Vacation / Travel): Toggle Pause Mode on or off to temporarily suspend all monitoring.

C. Testing Family Pairing (Guardian Mode & QR / Invite Code)
1. Open the "Family" tab (or go to Settings → Family & Authorization).
2. Tap the QR icon or "Invite Family Member" to generate a 48-hour pairing QR code and a 6-character alphanumeric invite code (e.g., CR-XXXXXX).
3. No camera or second physical device is required to test pairing: you can copy the 6-character invite code and enter it manually via "Scan Relative QR / Enter Code" on a second account, or inspect the pre-linked family members in the demo account.

3. PERMISSIONS & PRIVACY COMPLIANCE NOTES
• Location Permission (When In Use — Optional):
  Evernear NEVER performs continuous background GPS tracking. Location permission is optional and is only used to attach a single-shot coordinate when the user explicitly confirms a check-in or when an abnormal inactivity alert is triggered (configurable in Settings → Privacy & security → Location Data). The app functions fully even if Location permission is denied.
• Camera Permission (Optional):
  Used strictly for scanning a family member's pairing QR code. Users who decline camera permission can always pair manually using the 6-character text invite code.
• Microphone Permission:
  Never requested or used.

4. IN-APP ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Users can permanently delete their account and erase all associated personal data directly inside the app at any time via two convenient paths:
• Bottom Navigation → Settings → scroll down and tap "Delete Account" (red action button), OR
• Bottom Navigation → Settings → Privacy & security → "Delete Account".

5. NON-MEDICAL / NON-EMERGENCY DISCLAIMER (Guideline 1.4.1)
Evernear is a supplemental lifestyle and family check-in notification tool. It is not a medical device, does not perform clinical or health diagnostics, and does not contact public emergency services (911/112/999).
```
