1. What Evernear is

Evernear is a passive inactivity monitor and safety check-in system for solo dwellers, seniors, and loved ones living afar. It supports 1-tap 'I am OK' check-ins (weight 1.0), passive activity signals (0.2–0.8), and multi-channel guardian alerts.

2. Pairing and mutual consent

All relationships between Monitored users and Guardians in Evernear require voluntary, mutual consent via a 48-hour QR invitation. You must never use Evernear to monitor any person without their explicit knowledge and consent.

4. Medical disclaimer (Non-diagnostic)

Evernear is not a medical device and provides no medical advice.

The Composite Risk Score (0–100) and server monitoring states are strictly operational prioritization indicators based on check-in timing, not clinical diagnoses.
