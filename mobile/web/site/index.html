FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a phone Heartbeat have a weight of 0.0?

A charged phone sitting on a table with Wi-Fi does not prove its owner is safe. Evernear strictly separates technical network pings (Heartbeat = 0.0) from real human activity (0.2–0.8) and explicit 'I am OK' check-ins (1.0).

Will the app disturb seniors while they sleep at night?

No. With Quiet Hours (Sleep Windows), the server countdown automatically freezes during configured sleeping hours (e.g., 22:00 to 07:00 in the user's local timezone) and resumes smoothly in the morning.

How does location sharing work? Is GPS always on?

No. Evernear never tracks continuous background GPS. Location is captured as a single-shot coordinate only when you grant permission and perform a check-in or when an emergency alert triggers.

Is the Composite Risk Score (0–100) a medical diagnosis?

Not at all. The 0–100 score is a non-diagnostic operational prioritization indicator based on check-in latency and signal history to help family guardians prioritize attention. Evernear is not a medical device.

How do I permanently delete my account and personal data?

You can permanently delete your account and all personal data at any time directly inside the Evernear app (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion takes effect immediately on our servers. If you no longer have your device, use our public Delete Account web page (/delete-account).
