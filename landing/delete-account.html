Evernear Account Deletion Request

Account and Personal Data Deletion Request (GDPR Right to Erasure)

Using the Evernear app? You can permanently delete your account immediately inside the app at: Settings → Delete Account (which executes DELETE /api/v1/users/me immediately).
This web page is provided if you no longer have access to the app or your device.

How to request deletion via Web / Email

  • Send an email from the address associated with your Evernear account to support@erai.vn.
  • Use the subject line “Evernear Account Deletion Request” and include your display name. Never send your password or authentication tokens.
  • We verify account ownership via email, permanently erase your data, and confirm completion within 30 days.

Send Account Deletion Request Email

Data that will be permanently deleted

Your entire account, personal profile, registered devices, monitoring configurations, Quiet Hours schedules, check-in history, passive activity signals, single-shot locations, incident history, notification channels, and guardian relationships.

Data that may be temporarily retained

Minimal anonymized security audit logs may be retained where required by law for fraud prevention and service security. Pre-existing encrypted database backups are overwritten through standard rotation cycles.
