Privacy Policy

Applies to the Evernear mobile and web applications. Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Evernear is an inactivity monitor and safety check-in app for seniors and solo dwellers. Built on Privacy-by-Default: zero cameras, zero microphones, and zero continuous background GPS tracking. This policy explains what we collect, who can see it, and how you can permanently delete your data.

1. What we collect

  • Account and profile Email address, one-way bcrypt-hashed password (we never store plaintext passwords), display name, phone number (optional), avatar, preferred interface language, IANA timezone, and role (Monitored or Guardian).
  • Check-in and Passive Activity Signals In Monitored Mode, Evernear records explicit 'I am OK' check-ins (weight 1.0), and enabled passive activity events (screen unlock, charging, step count — weight 0.2 to 0.8). Technical device heartbeats (weight 0.0) record connectivity and battery status solely for diagnostics.
  • Location (Consent-gated single-shot only) Evernear never collects continuous background GPS. Single-shot coordinates are recorded only when you grant permission and explicitly check in or when an emergency incident escalates (`ALERT_TRIGGERED`) so your linked Guardians can assist you.
  • Device & Push Notification Information: FCM push tokens, operating system, app version, and alert delivery destinations (SMS / Telegram) configured by you.

2. Why we use your data

  • To operate the 8-state safety state machine — evaluating check-in deadlines, Grace Periods, and freezing countdowns during Quiet Hours.
  • To deliver multi-channel reminders and alerts — sending FCM push notifications, SMS.
  • To calculate the Operational Priority Risk Score (0–100) — helping Guardians prioritize response urgency (strictly non-medical and non-diagnostic).

We never sell your personal data, and we display zero advertisements.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your approved Guardians — only people who completed two-way pairing via a 48-hour QR invite can view your safety status and receive your alerts.
  • Essential technical infrastructure — push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and SMS / Telegram gateways to deliver emergency alerts.

4. Storage and Security

  • All communication between the mobile app, web browser, and servers is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS (WSS for WebSocket).
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt; sessions use short-lived JWT Access Tokens with rotating Refresh Tokens and device session validation.

5. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all personal data at any time directly inside the Evernear app at: Settings → Delete Account. This invokes DELETE /api/v1/users/me and takes effect immediately, erasing your profile, devices, check-ins, passive signals, locations, and guardian relationships. If you no longer have your device, submit a request via our public Evernear Account Deletion Request page.

If you have already uninstalled the app, you can submit a request at the Delete Account Request (/delete-account) page or email support@erai.vn.

6. Contact

For any questions regarding privacy and personal data protection, please contact: support@erai.vn.
